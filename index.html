PIP’S JOURNEY · CHAPTER 01

The Lantern Gate

At the edge of Lantern Valley stood a gatehouse with a bell that had not rung for a hundred years. Pip had promised to carry a little light to the lighthouse before dusk. The old owl keeper watched as Pip reached for the brass bell. One clear note, Pip thought, might be enough to wake the island.

PIP’S NOTEBOOK

“The map says the road begins where the bell can be heard. I hope it still remembers its song.”
